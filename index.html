A MORE INFORMED NEXT STEP

Start with understanding, not assumptions.

Reproductive health questions can feel difficult to navigate. ReproGuide is designed to make general information easier to understand and help you prepare for appropriate professional care.

If you are unsure about an answer, you can leave it undisclosed. Missing information should never be treated as proof that a health concern is absent.

!

An important note about your health

ReproGuide is an educational prototype, not a diagnostic tool. It cannot diagnose infertility or other medical conditions, determine your fertility, or replace an assessment by a qualified healthcare professional. Its guidance is educational and should not be treated as a medical conclusion.

YOUR PRIVACY MATTERS

Your answers stay in the current session.

The prototype is designed to keep assessment answers in application memory rather than save them persistently in your browser or send them to a server. Refreshing or closing the page may clear your answers.

This describes the intended behaviour of the prototype. Technical verification is required as features are implemented.
